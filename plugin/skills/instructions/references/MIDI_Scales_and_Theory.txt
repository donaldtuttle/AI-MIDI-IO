### **1. Understanding Musical Scales**

#### **What is a Scale?**

A **scale** is a sequence of musical notes ordered by pitch. Scales are the foundation of melodies and harmonies in music, and they provide the structure for both simple and complex compositions.

Major scale intervals: W W H W W W H
Natural minor: W H W W H W W
